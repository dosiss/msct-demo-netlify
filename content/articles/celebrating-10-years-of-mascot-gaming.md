What started as a small indie studio with a single, promising game set has grown into a brand recognized around the world. Over the past decade, we’ve expanded our portfolio with diverse and exciting titles, reaching players across the globe.

Through it all, we’ve stayed true to our core belief: quality over quantity. We focus on creating games that are thoughtfully designed, enjoyable, and memorable—because for us, game creation is about more than just numbers. It’s about crafting experiences that players genuinely connect with.

We’re proud of how far we’ve come, but we’re even more excited about what’s ahead. Every new project is a chance to explore, experiment, and push our creative boundaries, while staying true to the principles that got us here.

Thank you to our players, partners, and the amazing team behind Mascot Gaming. Here’s to the next 10 years of making games that inspire and entertain.
