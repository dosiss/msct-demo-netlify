We’ve recently returned from SiGMA Central Europe 2025 in Rome — a few focused and productive days that concluded our exhibition year on a confident note.

Our appreciation goes to the SiGMA team for the well-organized event and the professional environment that encouraged meaningful discussions and new ideas.

![](../../images/img_blog_sigma-2025-finale-12.jpg)

This year is a significant one for us — Mascot Gaming marks its 10th anniversary. The design of our booth reflected this milestone. We wanted it to convey our evolution as a company and the creative identity shaped through our top games. The result combined recognisable visual elements with new details that represented where we are today.

![](../../images/img_blog_sigma-2025-finale-2.jpg)

We’d like to thank our design team and construction partner for their thoughtful and precise work. Their attention to detail helped bring our concept to life in a way that felt both distinctive and true to Mascot’s spirit.

Among the memorable moments of the event was our Scratch the Bank Tournament, which drew plenty of attention and friendly competition. Congratulations to Olga Gavrilyuk from Red Star Casino, whose sharp play earned her the win — and an iPhone prize.

![](../../images/img_blog_sigma-2025-finale-3.jpg)

SiGMA Central Europe was a fitting conclusion to a busy and rewarding year. We had the opportunity to meet partners, exchange ideas, and present our latest releases.

As we look ahead to 2026, we’re preparing to share our updated roadmap — outlining upcoming projects and new directions for Mascot Gaming.

![](../../images/img_blog_sigma-2025-finale-4.jpg)
