**Features**

**Free Spins**

![](../../images/img_blog_tessatomb-2.jpg)

Free Spins are triggered when at least 3 Scatter symbols land anywhere on the reels. Once activated, the feature shifts control to the player: you choose one of three Free Spins modes — 25 spins with x2 multiplier, 10 spins with x5, or 5 spins with x10. 

![](../../images/img_blog_tessatomb-3.jpg)

Additional 3+ Scatters can extend any of these routes, offering 5, 10, or even 25 extra spins depending on where the player has chosen to go. No mode is inherently superior — the game hands over control, and the outcome is shaped by willingness to push deeper.

**Risk & Buy**

![](../../images/img_blog_tessatomb-4.jpg)

Risk & Buy remains one of the most defining mechanics in Mascot Gaming’s catalogue, and here it plays to the theme perfectly. After a winning spin, players may **risk their payout in pursuit of a multiplier — up to x1000** — or bypass luck entirely by buying direct access to Free Spins. Prices update dynamically. The feature never feels like a background addition — it is integrated as a moment of confidence testing: follow instinct or secure progress.

![](../../images/img_blog_tessatomb-5.jpg)

**Theme & Design**

_Tessa Hunt and the Warrior Tomb_ embraces a bright, vivid adventure theme. The jungle setting is saturated and dense, and torches light the entryway into the tomb. . Tessa herself stands out immediately: expressive, steady. 

Symbols reinforce the tone through variety and clear visual identity — carved masks with glowing eyes, jaguar heads, shields, blades and geometric emblems. Everything aligns stylistically: rich colors, natural textures and sharp contrasts keep the screen visually active and easy to read. The atmosphere feels adventurous but not chaotic — more like a guided expedition than a chaotic treasure hunt.

Music supports this direction without overpowering it. The sound is energetic enough to maintain momentum and set the mood, complementing the visuals.

**Where Tessa Leads, Fortune Follows**

_Tessa Hunt and the Warrior Tomb_ leaves the impression of a slot that values active engagement over passive spinning. It gives the player space to choose how deep to go, how bold to be, and when to move forward. The experience doesn’t rely on spectacle to hold attention. Instead, it offers a structured, confident flow where risk, tempo and atmosphere work together.
