We’re thrilled to announce that Mascot Gaming has won the Game Music / Soundtrack of the Year at the prestigious SBC Awards Europe!

This incredible milestone is a moment of pride for our entire team — and especially for our composer, Max Shulgin, whose talent, creativity, and passion have brought our games to life through unforgettable music.

At Mascot Gaming, we create all our music in-house, with full dedication to detail, emotion, and immersion. Our soundtracks have been praised across the industry for their originality and high production value — and now, we’ve been officially recognized with an award that celebrates exactly that.

This win is a celebration of our commitment to crafting immersive, high-quality audio experiences that elevate gameplay and connect with players on a deeper level.

We’d like to thank the SBC Awards Europe jury, our partners, and most importantly — our players, who continue to inspire us to push boundaries with every beat.
