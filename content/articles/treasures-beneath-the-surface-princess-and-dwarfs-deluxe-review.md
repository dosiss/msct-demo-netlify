Some tales begin with a whisper — deep underground, where the light of lanterns meets the glimmer of hidden gems. _Princess and Dwarfs Deluxe_ by Mascot Gaming draws players into such a world: mysterious, radiant, and full of promise. It’s a story where courage meets curiosity, and every spin uncovers a new layer of the unknown.

Just like Mascot’s colorful [**candy slot**](https://mascot.games/the-candy-slot-deluxe), this game offers a distinct tone and pacing — yet instead of sugar and sparkle, it’s built on stone, gold, and quiet determination.

**Technical Information**

The game is set on a 6×6 grid with the flexible Rockways mechanic, offering anywhere from 64 to 46,656 ways to win. With high volatility and a 25.93% hit rate, the gameplay feels dynamic and deliberate, maintaining a steady rhythm between smaller wins and moments of real impact. The maximum win potential reaches x20,000 of the total bet, supported by smooth animation and consistent performance across all platforms.

**Features**

In Princess and Dwarfs Deluxe, wins evolve naturally through the Rockfall Feature. When symbols form a winning combination, they disappear, allowing new ones to fall from above. With each Rockfall, the multiplier increases — up to x5 in the main game and up to x15 during Free Spins.

![](../../images/img_blog_princessdwarfs-2.jpg)

The **Mystery Symbols** define much of the game’s dynamic. They act as both Wilds and Scatters: completing lines when needed and triggering Free Spins when four or more appear. Once Free Spins begin, these symbols expand into full **six-cell Wild reels**.

![](../../images/img_blog_princessdwarfs-3.jpg)

The **Risk & Buy Feature** introduces player choice into the structure. Free Spins can be purchased directly — either for a standard price or a dynamic one that changes its value after each spin. 

![](../../images/img_blog_princessdwarfs-4.jpg)

With the **Risk Game**, players can risk their winnings for a chance to multiply them up to **x1000**. Designed as a Wheel of Fortune with three levels, the **Risk Game** offers different multipliers at each stage.

![](../../images/img_blog_princessdwarfs-5.jpg)

**Theme and Design**

The visual direction of _Princess and Dwarfs Deluxe_ relies on contrast and small details. The main game takes place in a mine, surrounded by wood, stone, and glowing crystals. Lanterns sway lightly, reflections move across surfaces, and the whole scene feels crafted rather than decorated. The design avoids excess — every element has a clear purpose.

When Free Spins start, the reels move to the Princess’s chamber. The atmosphere becomes warmer and brighter, built on soft lighting and gold accents. This shift creates a sense of transition — from work to discovery, from hidden effort to visible reward.

The music has a cheerful, fairy-tale tone. It’s playful and kind, creating a sense of motion and purpose without rushing the pace. Together with the visuals, it gives the game a personality that’s charming, clear, and unmistakably its own.

**No Dust, Just Diamonds**

_Princess and Dwarfs Deluxe_ continues Mascot Gaming’s approach to building structured, atmospheric slots. It combines evolving gameplay with a clear visual identity, where every mechanic supports the mood rather than competing with it. The Rockways system, growing multipliers, and distinctive music create a steady rhythm that feels consistent from the first spin to the last.

Players who enjoyed the bright and lively mood of the [**candy slot**](https://mascot.games/the-candy-slot-deluxe) will find _Princess and Dwarfs Deluxe_ a different kind of delight — grounded, detailed, and full of quiet rewards. It’s a confident, well-composed release — measured, engaging, and unmistakably Mascot in spirit
