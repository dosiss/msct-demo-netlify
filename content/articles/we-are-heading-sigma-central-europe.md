From November 3–6, the Mascot Gaming team will take part in SiGMA Central Europe — one of the key international events in the iGaming industry. This year, the exhibition takes place in Rome, bringing together game developers, operators, and technology leaders from around the world.

You can find us at **BOOTH 3237 G**. We’ll be showcasing our latest releases, sharing insights, and discussing new opportunities with partners. Visitors will also have the chance to meet our business development and account management teams in person and experience the distinct Mascot Gaming style that defines our work.

This year is particularly meaningful for us, as Mascot Gaming celebrates its 10th anniversary. We look forward to marking this milestone at SiGMA together with our colleagues and partners — a great reason to stop by and celebrate with us.

And there’s one more reason to visit our booth — the **Scratch the Bank Tournament**, inspired by the adventurous spirit of Rome.

From November 4–6, guests can join the game and compete for the main prize — a brand-new iPhone. To take part, simply visit **BOOTH 3237 G**, register for the tournament, and claim 15 free spins to score as many points as possible. The winner will be announced on November 6 at 3:00 PM at our booth.
