Mascot Gaming expands its Rockblocks portfolio with Spicy Candies Rockblocks, the second game to feature this innovative mechanic. Unlike traditional paylines, Rockblocks reward players for forming groups of adjacent matching symbols, creating an engaging chain reaction of wins. This [candy slot](https://mascot.games/the-candy-slot-deluxe) has a lot to offer.

![](../../images/img_blog_spicycandies-1.jpg)

**TECHNICAL INFORMATION**

Set on a 6x6 grid, Spicy Candies Rockblocks brings a unique payout system where Rockblocks of five or more matching symbols—trigger wins. The game features a hit frequency of 34.6%, ensuring frequent payouts. With low volatility, it’s perfect for players who enjoy consistent and steady gameplay. The maximum win multiplier reaches x3500, offering enticing winning potential despite its casual-friendly volatility.

**FEATURES**

In Spicy Candies Rockblocks, each win activates the Rockfall Feature, removing winning symbols and allowing new ones to drop into place, potentially creating consecutive wins. With each successive Rockfall, the win multiplier increases, reaching up to x25.

![](../../images/img_blog_spicycandies-3.jpg)

The Coin Feature introduces coin symbols. A combination of 5+ Coin Symbols awards direct prizes, providing additional winning opportunities beyond standard payouts.

![](../../images/img_blog_spicycandies-4.jpg)

For players who enjoy taking chances, the Risk Game allows winnings to be gambled for a potential multiplier boost, giving control over how far they want to push their luck. This thrilling Wheel of Fortune-inspired game features three levels, each offering higher multipliers—up to a staggering x1000—but also greater risks.

![](../../images/img_blog_spicycandies-5.jpg)
