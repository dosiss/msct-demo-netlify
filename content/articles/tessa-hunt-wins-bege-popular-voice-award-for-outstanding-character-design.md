Mascot Gaming won the Popular Voice Award for Outstanding Character Design at BEGE (Balkan Entertainment & Gaming Expo) 2025.

From the very early stages of development, it was clear to us that Tessa Hunt would connect with players. Confident, determined, and always drawn to adventure, she quickly became a character with strong presence and personality. Players have already seen her different sides in Temple of Horus and The Warrior Tomb, and she will return once again in our upcoming release planned for early 2026 — Mission Monte Carlo.

Iconic characters have always been a key part of Mascot Gaming’s creative approach. This award, especially in the Popular Voice category, is a great confirmation that players feel that connection too.

A big thank you to everyone who voted for us, and a special thanks to our amazing team for their fantastic work and talent! ❤️
