We’re excited to share great news for our partners and players in Latvia — Mascot Gaming has significantly expanded its certified portfolio in this regulated market. A total of 48 games are now officially approved for operation in Latvia, offering even more high-quality entertainment to local audiences. The certification was conducted by Global Lab, an internationally recognized testing laboratory. Their thorough assessment ensures our games fully comply with the technical and regulatory standards required in Latvia — giving our partners complete confidence in the content they integrate.

**Nikolai Dremov, Business Development Manager at Mascot Gaming:**

“We’re thrilled to offer an even broader selection of our games in Latvia. Expanding our certified portfolio allows us to bring more value and flexibility to operators working in regulated jurisdictions.”
