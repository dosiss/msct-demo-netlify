2025 - what a year!

We released 38 games, continuing to grow, experiment, and refine what we do. A special moment this year was the launch of TTT, our new brand, which went live in January 2025 and marked a new chapter for the team.

Throughout the year, we stayed closely connected to the industry, taking part in major events and staying in constant dialogue with the market. Along the way, we formed many new partnerships and strengthened relationships we truly value.

Receiving two important awards made the year even more special. We were honored with Best Soundtrack at the SBC Awards, and the BeGe Popular Choice Award for Outstanding Character Design — recognition that means a lot to everyone involved in creating our games.

And all of this happened in the year Mascot Gaming turned 10. A big milestone, and without a doubt, a magical year for us.

A heartfelt thank you to our team, our partners, and our players for your trust, support, and energy. We couldn’t have done it without you. 💛
