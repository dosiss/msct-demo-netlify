**Features**

**Free Spins**

Free Spins trigger when 3, 4, or 5 Scatter symbols land anywhere on the reels. The feature awards 15, 20, or 25 spins, depending on how many Scatters appeared. Every win during Free Spins is multiplied by x3 — the multiplier applies to all payouts, with no exceptions or symbol conditions to track.

The round can continue if additional Scatters land. A new set of 3, 4, or 5 Scatters retriggers the feature and adds the same number of spins again. The mechanic is simple: entry, multiplier, retrigger.

**Risk & Buy**

The developer’s signature Risk and Buy feature gives players control over pacing.

**Buy** allows an immediate entry into Free Spins instead of waiting for Scatters to land naturally. The price may be fixed or offered dynamically after a spin, depending on the state of the game.
